# Northwind Corp — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

**Classification:** Internal  
**Department:** Human Resources  
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Leave requests require manager approval at least 5 business days in advance, except for emergencies.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to **10 sick days** per year with self-certification. More than 3 consecutive sick days require a medical note.

## PII and privacy

Employees must not store **personally identifiable information (PII)** on personal USB drives or public cloud folders. Customer PII may only be accessed through approved CRM systems with **role-based access control (RBAC)**.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews run **twice per year** (June and December). Ratings feed into bonus pools and development plans.

## Remote work

Remote employees must use the corporate **VPN** when accessing internal tools. Devices must have disk encryption enabled.

## Code of conduct

Harassment, data leakage, and sharing credentials are grounds for disciplinary action up to termination.
