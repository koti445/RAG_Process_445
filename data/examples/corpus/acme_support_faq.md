# ACME Gadgets — Support FAQ (practice corpus)

## Warranty

All ACME laptops include a **24-month warranty** from the purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Accidental damage is not covered unless you purchased ACME Care Plus.

## Returns

You may return unopened items within **30 days** for a full refund. Opened items can be exchanged within 14 days with proof of purchase.

## Password reset

To reset your ACME account password, open the login page, click **Forgot password**, and follow the email link. Links expire after 60 minutes.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping (2 days) is available at checkout for an extra fee.
